About the hotel

A quiet corner of Gulberg

MaryLeena Hotel Gulberg is a 3-star hotel on a tree-lined street in Block P, Gulberg II — one of Lahore's most central neighbourhoods. The city's best-known food street, MM Alam Road, and the shops of Liberty Market are both close by, while the hotel itself stays peaceful and unhurried.

A 24-hour front desk, an on-site concierge and room service mean help is always at hand, whether you are in Lahore for business, a family visit, or a weekend in the city.
